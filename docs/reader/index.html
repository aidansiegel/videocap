Videocap light reader

On the station, press the power button twice for its status, or three times for its address. Hold the phone a hand's width from the green light.

The camera starts when you press Start.

Message

If it doesn't read
  • Come closer, so the light is bigger than a grain of rice on the screen, and hold still.
  • If it locks onto the wrong spot, tap the green light in the picture.
  • Each message starts with a one-second flash. If you started late, press the button twice again.
  • By eye: it's Morse at 12 words a minute, after the long flash, ending with .-.-..
