Help

  • Why it records before you press play: a camcorder or VCR sends no picture until its tape moves, so a recording started by the picture would miss the first seconds. While it waits, videocap records in standby: press play while the ring is green. If standby has run out, press REC, then play.
  • Naming captures: type a title (and anything else you know) under Next capture, or load your list with Item list and each capture takes the next item. You can fix the name while it records: the files are renamed when it finishes.
  • Watching captures: flip the Video selector over the picture to Captures. The newest plays first; click a capture to play it, then flip back to Live.
  • The picture dropped out right after play, so you started again: nothing to do. A very short take counts as a false start: it's held, and the item number stays the same.
  • A take looks or sounds bad (lost color, tracking, silence): press Bad take on it, pick that item again, clean the heads if needed, and capture it again. Set-aside masters move to a discarded folder; nothing is deleted.
  • "Not recording: only … GB free": there's no room for a master. Make room on the masters disk or swap in another capture disk, then press REC.
  • Nothing happens with the source on: check the picture cable, that it's playing (a camcorder in playback mode) and the input setting, then turn it off and on.

Item list

Paste cells from a spreadsheet (with a header row such as Number, Title, Format, Date, Notes), a CSV, or one title per line. Captures are named from it in order.
