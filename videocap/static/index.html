Help
  • Why it records before you press play: a camcorder or VCR sends no picture until its tape moves, so a recording started by the picture would miss the first seconds. While it waits, videocap records in standby: press play while the ring is green. If standby has run out, press REC, then play.
  • Wrong item: before pressing play, pick the right one under Next item. If a take already got the wrong number, rename its files afterwards.
  • The picture dropped out right after play, so you started again: nothing to do. A very short take counts as a false start: it's held, and the item number stays the same.
  • A take looks or sounds bad (lost colour, tracking, silence): press Bad take on its label, pick that item again, clean the heads if needed, and capture it again. Set-aside masters move to a discarded folder; nothing is deleted.
  • "Not recording: only … GB free": there's no room for a master. Make room on the masters disk or swap in another capture disk, then press REC.
  • Nothing happens with the source on: check the picture cable, that it's playing (a camcorder in playback mode) and the input setting, then turn it off and on.
